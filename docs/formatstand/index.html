What it does

FormatStand is a Dashwell LLC Mac app, available now on the App Store. A local-first batch converter for musicians and archivists who work in folders — not one file at a time.

Batch convert folders

Drag in files, folders, or both. Output to AAC (M4A), Apple Lossless, AIFF, or WAV — beside the source or into a remembered folder.

Keep tags & artwork

Title, artist, album, track numbers, and embedded art come across. Missing cover? FormatStand uses cover.jpg or folder.jpg beside the audio.

Split CUE albums

Read a .cue sheet next to a CD rip, show one row per track, and cut each track at its INDEX point — named and tagged from the sheet.

Extract audio from video

Drop a MOV, MP4, M4V, 3GP, or 3G2 and convert its audio track — interviews, lectures, concert clips without the video.

Local-first, offline

No accounts, no analytics, no network access. Files are read only where you point the app. Originals are never overwritten.
