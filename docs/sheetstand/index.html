SheetStand

Sheet music library & performance viewer

iPad-first · Mac & iPhone · Under development

Scan your PDF sheet music, organize by title and composer, and play full-screen at the piano.

Coming soon

SheetStand is a Dashwell LLC app in active development. A local-first catalog for musicians who keep their library as PDFs — not another cloud subscription.

PDF library scan

Point SheetStand at a folder of sheet-music PDFs. It builds a searchable catalog on your device.

Title, composer & key

On-device extraction fills metadata so you can find the right piece fast — with a review step when heuristics need a hand.

Full-screen performance view

iPad-first viewer with tap page turns, keep-awake, and pinch zoom — built for the music stand, not the couch.

Local-first

Your scores stay on your device. Optional iCloud Drive sync is planned for a later release — off by default when it ships.

Mac, iPad & iPhone

One native SwiftUI codebase. iPad is the primary stage; Mac and iPhone share the same library tools.

From Dashwell LLC

Built by the same team as Dashwell’s privacy-first money apps — a separate product for musicians, not a finance feature.
